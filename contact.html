Before you write

Scientific collaboration

The group works on wave-optics lensing, dark-matter substructure, black-hole binaries and gravitational-wave propagation. My own time is very limited, so if your question falls squarely in one person's area it is usually faster and more productive to write to them directly — the group page lists who works on what. Otherwise, a paragraph on what you have in mind and which of our papers it connects to is the most useful opening.

Prospective students and postdocs

See joining the group — it covers advertised positions, fellowship applications and Master's projects.

Seminar and conference invitations

Very welcome. Please include the date, format, audience level and whether the talk will be recorded. Recorded talks from previous years are on the Talks page.

Media inquiries

Happy to comment on gravitational waves, lensing, dark matter and dark energy, in English, Spanish or German. For institutional coordination, the AEI press office can be reached through the institute website. Previous coverage is collected under press and media.
