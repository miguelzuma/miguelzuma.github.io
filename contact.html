Before you write

Scientific collaboration

I am interested in collaborations on wave-optics lensing, dark-matter substructure, black-hole binaries and gravitational-wave propagation. A paragraph on what you have in mind, and which of our papers it connects to, is the most useful opening.

Prospective students and postdocs

Funded positions are advertised on INSPIRE-HEP and applications go through the advert. Brief expressions of interest and pertinent questions are welcome, though I rarely have time to discuss research directions before the interview stage, so please do not count on a reply. The joining the group section covers the other routes: external fellowships, for which I can support a small number of applications each year, and Master's projects.

Seminar and colloquium invitations

Very welcome. Please include the date, format, audience level and whether the talk will be recorded. Recorded talks from previous years are on the Talks & Outreach page.

Media enquiries

Happy to comment on gravitational waves, lensing, dark matter and dark energy, in English, Spanish or German. For institutional coordination, the AEI press office can be reached through the institute website. Previous coverage is collected under press and media.
