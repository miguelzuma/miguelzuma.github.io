The work runs from wave-optics theory through public software to the analysis of real gravitational-wave and cosmological data.

The research programme

Most of the group's effort goes into one connected problem: what the lensing of waves can tell us about the dark contents of the Universe.

Gravitational lensing of waves

Diffraction and interference imprint the mass distribution of a lens directly on a gravitational-wave signal. We build the theory and the methods that read it back out.

Dark matter and compact structures

Lensing is one of the few probes sensitive to dark matter on sub-galactic scales, where the leading candidates disagree most sharply.

Black holes and strong gravity

Black holes both bend light and radiate. We use them as lenses, as sources, and as laboratories for gravity in the strong field.

Methods, inference and software

Fast wave-optics solvers, statistical searches and the public codes GLoW and hi_class — so the results are reproducible by anyone.
