Press and media

Black holes as telescopes (2026)

Our work on discovering supermassive black-hole binaries through quasi-periodic lensed starlight was an Editors' Suggestion in Physical Review Letters, with press releases from the Albert Einstein Institute and the University of Oxford. It was covered by Phys.org, myScience, ScienMag and the LISA Newsletter.

ERC Consolidator Grant (2025)

The GLOW award was announced by the Albert Einstein Institute and the Max Planck Society. In the same year I acted as expert advisor for the Kurzgesagt video Cosmic Crisis (sources), and was interviewed by El País for ¿Podremos viajar en el tiempo?

Wave-optics lensing and dark energy (2023–2025)

The group's work on wave-optics lensing appeared in the Max Planck Society 2023 Yearbook. The project With Einstein on Crooked Paths was funded by Max Planck supporting members as the 2024 Annual Project. Our model-independent reconstruction of dark-energy evolution was an Editors' Suggestion in Physical Review D and featured by the AEI. Our analysis of LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA data was covered by Astrobites, and our work on strong-field effects on gravitational-wave propagation by the AEI.

Gravitational-wave lensing beyond Einstein's theory (2020)

An Editors' Suggestion in Physical Review D, featured by the Albert Einstein Institute and the University of Chicago, and covered by Forbes, The Debrief and Europa Press.

Primordial black holes and dark matter (2018)

Our limit on compact objects as dark matter was an Editors' Suggestion in Physical Review Letters with an APS Physics Viewpoint. It was discussed in UC Berkeley News, Wired, New Scientist, The Daily Californian, Forbes and CEA (in French). The paper's title also inspired an interview in El Confidencial and a video with 2.2 million views (both in Spanish).

Dark energy after GW170817 (2017)

An Editors' Suggestion in Physical Review Letters with an APS Physics Viewpoint. Covered in Quanta Magazine and Wired, Science News, New Scientist, Forbes, PNAS Inner Workings, Berkeley Lab News and CEA-IPhT (in French).

Also: a community profile in the EuCAPT blog (2022), and a feature among the best Swedish space-science research of 2015 (in Swedish).

Career advice

Drawing on experience as both an applicant and a reviewer, I wrote Get your application noticed for the Berkeley Science Review — practical advice on writing academic applications. I have served on the NASA Hubble Fellowship Program panel, the FCT individual support call in Portugal, and the AEI committee that reviews around 150 postdoctoral applications per year.
