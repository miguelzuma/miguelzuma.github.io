Joining the group

Postdoctoral researchers

Positions funded through the GLoW ERC Consolidator Grant are advertised as they open. I am also glad to host applicants for external fellowships — Humboldt, Marie Skłodowska-Curie, Margarita Salas and similar schemes — and the group has hosted several successfully. Get in touch well before the deadline so we can develop a project together.

PhD students

Doctoral positions at the AEI are normally filled through the IMPRS on Gravitational-Wave Astronomy and the institute's annual call. Mention my name and a specific interest in your application. Co-supervision with other institutions is also possible and has worked well in the past.

Master's and undergraduate projects

I regularly supervise Master's theses and summer internships, often jointly with a home-university supervisor. Projects tend to be computational and connected to an ongoing paper. Write with a short description of your background and what you would like to work on.

Visitors

Short and long research visits are welcome, and several have led to joint publications. The group can sometimes contribute to local costs. Please propose concrete dates and a project idea.
