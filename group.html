Joining the group

Funded positions are advertised publicly and applications go through the advert. Brief questions are welcome; a reply is not always possible.

Funded positions

Postdoctoral and doctoral positions supported by the group's projects are advertised on INSPIRE-HEP. That is where to look, and applying through the advert is what counts. A brief expression of interest or a specific question about a position is welcome — but please do not count on a reply, as I rarely have time to discuss research directions before the interview stage.

Fellowship applications

I can support a limited number of applications for external fellowships each year: Marie Skłodowska-Curie and Juan de la Cierva for postdoctoral researchers, FPU and “la Caixa” for doctoral students. These need a project developed jointly and well ahead of the deadline, so the number I can take on is genuinely small.

Master's theses

A thesis is usually attached to an ongoing project in the group. Most are computational, though theory projects are also possible. Students of the IFT master's program are given priority.

Visits

Research visits normally grow out of an existing collaboration, and several have led to joint publications — see the visitors above.
