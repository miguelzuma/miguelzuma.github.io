{%- comment -%} Escape a string from _data for LaTeX: {% include tex-escape.html s=some.string %} Order matters. Braces and the dollar sign are escaped before we introduce any of our own, and a literal backslash is parked on a placeholder so the escapes we add are not escaped a second time. The middle dot is the separator the data files use; LaTeX wants it as math. The brace-bearing replacements come from _data/tex.yml — Liquid ends a {{ ... }} expression at the first "}", so they cannot be written here literally. Accented letters, dashes and curly quotes need no help: inputenc and the T1 font encoding handle them. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = site.data.tex -%} {{- include.s | replace: '\', t.backslash_placeholder | replace: t.open_brace, t.escaped_open | replace: t.close_brace, t.escaped_close | replace: '$', '\$' | replace: t.ampersand, t.escaped_ampersand | replace: '%', '\%' | replace: '#', '\#' | replace: '_', '\_' | replace: t.tilde, t.escaped_tilde | replace: t.caret, t.escaped_caret | replace: t.backslash_placeholder, t.escaped_backslash | replace: '·', '$\cdot$' -}}
